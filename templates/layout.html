How to read this page

Each topic page has four columns. Requirements are the things you want, most important at the top. Non-requirements are the things you do not care about either way. Anti-requirements are the things you actively want to avoid. Suggested items are the candidates, best match first.

Drag a requirement by its handle to reorder it, or to move it between the first three columns. Every item is rescored and resorted as you do it, so two people with the same data and different priorities get different answers.

Press an item to see the score behind every requirement, along with any comment explaining it and the sources it was taken from. The bookmark toggle on a requirement insists on it: items that do not fully meet a forced requirement are struck through and pushed down the list.

A requirement carrying an info button was graded against a written rule. Press it to read the rule, because one candidate's 0.7 and another's do not mean the same thing until the rule says what they do.
